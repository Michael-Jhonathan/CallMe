# Welcome to Beta 1.0!

This is the first major update to CallMe!  
Here are all the new features in the beta version:

- P2P connection available with IPv6 and WebRTC for audio calls.
- Fallback to establish and maintain calls.
- Simple customization of your APP's interface.
- Ability to invite friends to your server via QR Code.
- Improved call structure management.
- Server propagation between members.
- Call encryption — only members of the call have access to what is being said.

- **Extremely low latency** in scenarios using IPv6 and WebRTC.

## Important!

The servers you create or participate in as a member are completely decentralized. All members of the server have identical copies on their devices, but don't worry, this is extremely lightweight!

As you might imagine, having these decentralized servers prevents us from exchanging messages in real time. For now, only voice calls are available.
